About Memor-ii

Memor-ii is an Interarts performance conceived at the Institute for Applied Creativity at Texas A&M. The program features live music performed by Lynn Vartan for percussion and electronics with interactive visualization faculty members Michael Bruner, Jinsil Hwaryoung Seo and Tim Weaver.

I was responsible for creating a number of the visual landscapes for the performance. Additionally, I created the TouchDesigner system to synchronize and playback the entire 1 hour performance.
