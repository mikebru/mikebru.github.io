Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy centers on the integration of creative technology, design thinking, and hands-on experimentation. I believe that students learn best when they are actively engaged in creating meaningful projects that they are passionate about. I teach all levels of students from Freshman to Graduate students in the Visualization Program, and each course is scaffolded to build students into well rounded designers and problem solvers.

In my freshman courses (VIST 110), I focus on instilling a fundamental design thinking approach that prepares students to take on a variety of challenges going forward. Students in this course may go on to focus on Animation, Game Development, or Interactive Design so it is my job to equip them with the foundational knowledge of iterative design processes and human centered workflows. In Design Innovation, we focus on user research, creating informed hypotheses, building prototypes and creating documentation. These are skills that can be applied to almost any field and benefit all future projects they work on. Students focus on using Figma and the Adobe Suite to produce mockups and interactive prototypes to validate their hypotheses.

In my Sophomore courses (VIST 210), the key focus is on technical skill development and learning the fundamentals of Time Based and Interactive storytelling. Unreal Engine has been an important tool and technology for teaching this course because it has a wide application across disciplines - be it Game Development, Animation, Virtual Production, or Interactive Design. The course consists of two major individual projects, the first is a 1 minute long animated music video and the second is an Interactive Narrative in the form of a "Choose your own Adventure" game. The music video project covers the fundamentals of linear time based media such as working with cameras, lighting, rendering, animations, materials and 3D editors. With the Interactive Narrative, students get to learn how to build interactivity with blueprints, create User Interfaces, and wrestle with the challenges of user interaction. Both of these projects are individual so students can build up their technical and artistic skills before working on larger team projects.

With my Junior and Senior students, I focus on the team dynamics and creating polished portfolio worthy projects.

Key principles include fostering interdisciplinary approaches, encouraging iterative design processes, and promoting collaborative learning environments. Students are challenged to explore emerging technologies while maintaining a strong foundation in design fundamentals.

Through project-based learning, students develop not only technical skills but also critical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and the confidence to tackle complex, real-world challenges in interactive design and digital media.

Courses

Below are the courses I currently teach, along with links to download syllabi where available.

VIST 110

Design Innovation

Introduction to fundamental design issues, processes, and theories relevant to design resolution and the creation of new ideas for innovative design products and services by keeping human needs at the center of the development process; Overview of design research, prototyping, evaluation, and communication techniques to generate valuable insights for design innovation.

Key Topics

VIST 210

Time and Interaction

Students will learn the language of time-based and interactive storytelling, with a focus on the skills needed to build a strong narrative experience and highly effective visuals for use in linear and non-linear motion graphics, and interactive projects. Students are expected to produce their own creative artifacts that grapple with the techniques and concepts related to time and interaction.

VIST-210 Syllabus

Key Topics

Principles of Animation Linear Narratives Interactive Narratives Unreal Engine

VIST 205, 305, 405

Interactive Vertical Studio

Allows students to explore experiential and interactive design through the creation of a semester long project of their own design. We will focus equally on the design process as well as producing and deploying our designs to be engaged with. Projects can take the form of art installations, exhibitions, themed attractions, interior design, event design, activations, etc. We will emphasize the pre-visualization of spaces and use 3-D technology to make design choices prior to deployment. In this studio students will focus their energy on creating a substantive project that combines visual thinking, design research, multimodality, interactivity, and storytelling.

Studio Syllabus

Key Topics

Team Project Based Interactive Design Creative Technology Branding Experience Design

VIST 289

Special Topics in TouchDesigner

Introduction to the fundamentals and practices of real-time interactive multimedia production through the use of the industry leading software TouchDesigner. Creation of interactive graphics, procedural visuals, data-driven systems and control systems through the use of node based programming. Topics include projection mapping, using sensors, creating user interfaces, interoperability, optimization and rendering. The course will consist of individual assignments to learn fundamentals and techniques with a culminating project at the end of the course.

Interactive Virtual Environments

Languages and techniques useful for the creation of real time virtual environments; definition of formal scene description structures; modeling and transformation techniques; simulation techniques; behaviors and message passing; user interaction and animation; multiuser environments; creating virtual interfaces; scripting techniques.
